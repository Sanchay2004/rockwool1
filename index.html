How Demand Radar works

Signals are combined to support regional replenishment decisions

01 · Historical sales

Distributor sell-out and seasonality patterns.

02 · Inventory

Current stock, safety stock and replenishment cycles.

03 · Project pipeline

ECO360 registered projects and estimated material needs.

04 · Forecast

Regional demand estimate and suggested stock positioning.

Demand Radar is a proposed forecasting capability. A live model would combine historical sell-out, inventory, registered projects, seasonality and agreed external signals. Forecasts are recommendations, not guaranteed demand.
